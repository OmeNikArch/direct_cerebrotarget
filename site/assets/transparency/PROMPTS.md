# Промпты серии иллюстраций прозрачности

## Общий мастер-промпт

Create a refined minimalist 2.5D editorial illustration for a responsive landing-page bento card. Use a near-orthographic three-quarter view, frosted matte glass, milky translucent acrylic, softly rounded geometry, shallow restrained depth, high-key diffused studio lighting from the upper left, and very low contrast.

Output as a true transparent RGBA PNG. The canvas outside the objects must be fully transparent: no white or colored background, checkerboard, card container, rectangular surface, or baked-in rounded corners. Keep alpha edges clean, without white fringe, colored speckles, glow, or a rectangular halo. The image must composite cleanly on both `#FFFFFF` and `#E9EEFF`.

Use a square responsive composition with one compact, recognizable visual cluster, primarily in the lower-right or central area. Leave a quiet upper-left zone for HTML copy. Keep all essential elements inside the central 70% and retain 12–16% transparent margin. The subject must remain legible at 280 px wide and tolerate enlargement or partial cropping on desktop.

Visible objects must be 88–92% `#FFFFFF`, `#F4F6FA`, and `#E9EEFF`. Use `#0A238B` only for delicate low-opacity outlines, connectors, and edge definition. Use `#2F63F5` only as a barely perceptible optical highlight. Use `#FFD400` only where the subject specification explicitly requests it.

No text, letters, numbers, currency signs, labels, logos, watermarks, people, hands, devices, realistic UI, office scene, purple, violet, magenta, pink, cyan, green, orange, black, dark surfaces, gradient background, neon, glossy crystal, chrome, hard shadows, dramatic perspective, decorative particles, or extra objects.

## 01 — Dashboard access

A floating semi-transparent frosted-glass advertising dashboard panel. Inside it, show only two or three abstract unreadable chart shapes and a minimal navigation rail. Add exactly one microscopic `#FFD400` status dot. Keep the dashboard overwhelmingly pale and ensure the yellow dot is the only vivid accent.

## 02 — Approving changes

Two or three smooth overlapping translucent glass sheets or rounded geometric tiles, gently offset in depth to suggest reviewed stages and an agreed change. Add exactly one tiny `#FFD400` checkmark at the calm intersection of the layers. Avoid looking like a checkbox, app icon, document stack, or interface.

## 03 — Promotion consultation

One elegant transparent frosted-glass magnifying lens hovering above a sparse abstract network of thin low-opacity `#0A238B` lines and pale glass nodes. The lens gently clarifies one small part of the network. Add exactly one microscopic four-point `#FFD400` spark inside the lens.

## 04 — Always in touch

Three overlapping smooth matte-glass spheres or rounded nodes in white and `#E9EEFF`, arranged as a compact triangular cluster and connected by thin curved low-opacity `#0A238B` data lines. The composition suggests continuous communication and calm coordination. Use no yellow.

## 05 — Transparent expenses

An abstract analytical bar chart constructed from four or five overlapping translucent matte-glass columns. Most bars are white, `#F4F6FA`, and `#E9EEFF`. Exactly one narrow bar is matte `#FFD400`, non-glowing, and occupies less than 4% of the visible object area. Do not use axes, labels, numbers, coins, banknotes, or currency signs.
